# Intent: loss adjusters can see claim status

## Problem

Third-party loss adjusters ask the claims line for status on claims they are handling.

## Proposed outcome

An adjuster can see the status of the claims assigned to them, and no others.

## Affected users and systems

Loss adjusters, the claims line, the partner portal.

## Constraints

An adjuster is not a customer and has no customer login.
