# Intent: show when a claim is waiting on the customer

## Problem

A claim that needs a document from the customer shows "in review", so the customer waits for us while we wait for them.

## Proposed outcome

The customer can see that the claim is waiting on them.

## Affected users and systems

Customers, the portal, claims-api.

## Constraints

None beyond the existing ones.

## Open questions

None.
